Good to know

A little city sense

01

Tap to ride

OMNY accepts a contactless card or phone at subway turnstiles. Use the same payment method for each ride.

02

Wear walkable shoes

Even subway trips include a few city blocks. Bring celebration shoes separately if that feels easier.

03

Leave a buffer

Plan to reach the Marriage Bureau before 11:00 AM so security, elevators, and group gathering feel unhurried.

04

Watch the weather

October can be crisp or warm. Check the forecast and bring a layer or compact umbrella as needed.
